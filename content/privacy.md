This site is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. Here is the complete and honest picture of what it does with your data:

**This site collects nothing.** There are no analytics scripts, no tracking pixels, no cookies set by this site, no contact forms, and no accounts. There is no server-side code of mine running at all — every page is a pre-built HTML file.

**Hosting.** Pages are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log standard request data (IP address, user agent, requested URL) to operate the service. That logging is governed by [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement), not by me — I never see it.

**Email.** If you email me at darryn@darrynpasqua.com, I have your email address and whatever you wrote. I use it to reply to you and for nothing else. I don't add you to lists, and I don't share it.

**External links.** Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

**AI crawlers.** This site's [robots.txt](/robots.txt) explicitly welcomes answer-engine and user-triggered AI agents and opts out of training-only crawlers.

If this policy changes, this page will change. Last updated: August 27, 2026.
